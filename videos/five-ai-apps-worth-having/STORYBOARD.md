## Video direction

- palette system: frame.md BlockFrame. Grounds cycle one pastel per frame: F1 cream, F2 blue, F3 pink, F4 green, F5 yellow, F6 offwhite with a yellow star-burst, F7 black close-frame. Black 4px borders + 8px hard shadows on cards, 3px + 4px on chips/pills. Yellow is the CTA color.
- type: Inter 900 uppercase negative-tracked for app names and hook words; Space Grotesk 700 uppercase for pills, rank labels, mock UI chrome. Mock UI text (typed prompts) in Inter 500 sentence case inside white cards.
- consistent stage for the list (F2–F6): rank card tilted top-left ("#5"… "#1") + "AI APP" label-pill, app name as the hero headline under it, one demo card in the middle band, a chip row below it. Same positions every item so the countdown reads as one run; only the ground color and the demo card change.
- motion grammar: power3.out long-tail settles; spring-pop (back.out, small overshoot) only on rank cards, chips and stickers, as the style is playful. Each piece reveals on its spoken cue (see Scene lines). Holds stay still apart from a subtle jitter on one decoration.
- rhythm: F1 fast slams; F2–F5 the same brisk reveal cadence; F6 gets a bigger entrance (star-burst behind "#1") and a held read on the podcast payoff; F7 is the calm inverted close.
- captions: on, bottom ~17% reserved; all content above y=1600.
- negative list: no real app logos (names in type only), no rounded corners except pills and the deco dot, no blurred shadows, no purple-blue AI gradients, no bokeh. No front-load-then-freeze, no screensaver drift.
